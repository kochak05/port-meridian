Port Meridian is an original top-down 2D open-world crime game: explore a city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the police, and complete 3 missions (Courier Run, Getaway Car, Harbor Heist) with combat, traffic, pedestrians, a wanted level, HUD and minimap.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome.

## Controls

- WASD / arrows — walk or drive
- E — enter / exit a car
- F, J, X or click — shoot (aim with the mouse)
- Shift — sprint
- P — pause, Q — save, M — mute sound

## The story

Mara Voss, a courier driver in Port Meridian, owes money to the Harbor Kings crew and works it off job by job: M1 Courier Run, M2 The Getaway Car, M3 Harbor Heist. Finish all three and the debt is paid.
